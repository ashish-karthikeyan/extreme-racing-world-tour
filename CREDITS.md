The World Tour map's dotted world (`js/data/worldmap.js`) is made from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com) 1:110m land (public domain). The menu and Garage studio reflects [Studio Small 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09) by Poly Haven (CC0), `assets/hdri/studio.hdr`.

## Made in code (original)

The race-car livery colours, 56 city landmarks, country props, the cockpit, the showroom and all effects (weather, night lighting, particles, post-processing) are built from simple shapes and shaders in `js/render3d/`. Music and sound effects are synthesised in code.

From [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com), CC0 1.0 (https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/), resized and recompressed:

## Made in code (original), added with the new cars and road

The detailed open-wheel race car (body, wings, halo, tyres, brake discs, team liveries and sponsor decals), all traffic (cars, taxi, van, lorries, trailers, bus) and their logos and operator names (all fictional), the road "wear" layer (tyre marks, patches, cracks, manhole and drain covers, puddle spots), worn lane lines, kerbs and flower beds are generated by the game's own code.
