## Short description (2-3 sentences)

Extreme Racing: World Tour is a fast 3D arcade racer that runs right in your browser. Weave through traffic from Sydney to Abu Dhabi, chain near misses into combos, fire nitro and earn three stars in every city. Play with a keyboard, a controller or a touch screen.

## Long description

**Extreme Racing: World Tour** is an arcade racing game you play right in your browser, with no download.

Take an open-wheel race car on a World Tour of 22 cities, from the harbour in Sydney to a head-to-head Grand Final in Abu Dhabi. Every city has its own landmarks, light and weather: the yachts of Monte Carlo, the neon strip of Las Vegas at night, cherry blossom in Tokyo, sunset in Miami and rain in London.

The faster you go, the better it pays. Leave your lane at the last moment to score a near miss, keep them coming to build a combo, then grab a nitro canister and blast past everything on the road.

**Features**

- **World Tour:** 22 cities, each with 3 stars to earn and 3 missions to finish, and a Grand Final at the end
- **VS Computer:** race the CPU on Easy, Medium, Hard or Impossible
- **Daily Challenge:** the same city and the same traffic for everyone each day; keep your streak going
- **Endless:** how far can you go?
- **Multiplayer:** two players on one keyboard, split screen
- **Near misses and combos:** late dodges score big, and combos multiply your points
- **Pickups:** coins, nitro canisters, shields that save you from one crash, and bypass arrows that swing you round the car ahead on a side track
- **The Garage:** 11 fictional teams to unlock, each with 3 liveries, plus upgrades for Handling, Nitro, Magnet and Shield
- **Launch starts:** hold gas and brake on the grid for a flying start
- **Real 3D:** day and night, rain, neon, wet-road reflections and a crash cam
- **Three cameras:** overhead, chase and cockpit
- **Photo mode:** pause, fly the camera anywhere, add filters and save a picture
- **Test Drive:** a short guided first race that teaches everything in a minute
- **18 awards** to collect, plus stats for your best runs
- **Graphics presets** from Low to Max, and an Auto setting that picks the right one for your device
- **Original music**, and you can play your own songs from your device

**Controls**

Keyboard:
- Left / Right (or A / D): change lane
- Up (or W): gas
- Down (or S, or Space): brake
- Hold Up + Down on the grid: launch start
- Esc or P: pause. R: restart while paused. P while paused: photo mode
- C: camera menu. V: next camera. 1 / 2 / 3: pick a camera
- M: mute. F: fullscreen. N: next song
- Multiplayer: player 1 uses the arrow keys, player 2 uses W A S D

Controller:
- Left stick or D-pad left / right: change lane
- RT or A: gas. LT, B or X: brake
- Hold RT + LT on the grid: launch start
- Start: pause. X: restart while paused. Y while paused: photo mode
- Y or View: camera menu. LB / RB: next camera

Touch screen (phone or tablet):
- Tap the left or right half of the screen to change lane
- GAS and BRAKE pads at the bottom (hold both on the grid for a launch start)
- Camera button top left, pause button bottom right

All teams, cars, liveries and sponsors in the game are fictional. Made by Ashish.
